06 · Questions

Common questions

Are the businesses in the Work section real?

No. They're sample projects for made-up brands, built to show what a finished site looks like. Every menu, form and phone layout works, so try them yourself. Client projects will be added with the client's permission.

Do I own my website?

Yes. The code is yours, and the domain and hosting accounts are set up in your name. If you ever want someone else to work on the site, nothing stands in the way.

How long does a website take?

It depends on the size of the site and how quickly content and feedback come together. Your written estimate includes a schedule, so you'll know before we start.

What does it cost to keep a site running?

A simple site can often be hosted for free. You'll pay for your domain name, usually $10 to $20 a year, and for any changes you ask me to make, at the same hourly rate.

Can you work on my existing site?

Yes. I can fix, speed up or extend an existing site, or rebuild it if that's the better deal over time. We'll work out which on the intro call.

Who writes the words and supplies the photos?

You know your business best, so the words start with you. I'll shape them into clear pages, and use your own photos or properly licensed stock images.
