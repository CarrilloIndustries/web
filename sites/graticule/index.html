03 / Principles

Shipped in every app

Made to measure.

Six rules every Graticule app ships under, from the first commit to the last driver.

  • 01

    Open to the last line

    Every app, driver and decoder lives in a public repository under the MIT license. Audit it, fork it or ship it inside your own product.

  • 02

    Native and GPU-fast

    Native builds draw every waveform on the GPU, so a 50-million-point capture pans and zooms without a stutter.

  • 03

    Works with your gear

    SCPI, VISA, USBTMC and LXI drivers cover 140+ scopes, supplies, loads and meters. Keep the instruments you already paid for.

  • 04

    Captures stay on your disk

    Waveforms, logs and board files are plain files on your machine. Nothing leaves it unless you choose to share it.

  • 05

    Script it into CI

    Python bindings, a CLI and a headless mode let a CI job flash a board, capture its boot and fail the build on a glitch.

  • 06

    Plug in from a browser

    Trace, Probe, Wire and Flash also run in a browser tab over WebUSB and WebSerial, which helps on a locked-down lab PC.
